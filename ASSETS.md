# Asset Registry

This document catalogs all open-source art, audio, and 3D assets used in the Hybrid Tactical Puzzle-RPG project. All assets listed here comply with open-source and free-for-commercial-use licenses (e.g., CC0, CC-BY, MIT).

## 2D Paintings & Visuals (Masquerade Mode)
*Themes: Bold/abstract strokes, earthy natural landscapes, detailed figures, historical palettes, stylized graphic art, mythological themes, vibrant street art.*

| Asset Name | Source | Author | License | Usage / Theme |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Free Painted 2D Backgrounds](https://itch.io/queue/c/12308/free-painted-backgrounds) | Itch.io | Various Artists | CC0 | Subdued earthy tones, natural landscapes, subtle blues. Used for narrative scenes. |
| [Abstract Geometric Textures](https://opengameart.org/content/abstract-textures) | OpenGameArt.org | N/A | CC0 | Abstract geometric patterns and cubist-like elements for hidden visual anchors. |
| [Urban Landscape / Cyberpunk Backgrounds](https://ansimuz.itch.io/cyberpunk-street-environment) | Itch.io | Ansimuz | CC0 | Urban skylines at night, neon light installations, vibrant street art on brick walls. |
| [Classical Building Architecture](https://opengameart.org/content/lpc-roman-architecture) | OpenGameArt.org | bluecarrot16 | CC-BY 3.0 | Grand, classic historical architecture for narrative exploration. |
| [Mythological & Allegorical Portraits](https://opengameart.org/content/rpg-portraits) | OpenGameArt.org | Various | CC-BY | Detailed figures and rich historical palettes for character portraits and gods. |
